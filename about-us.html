WHO WE ARE

Plousios Engineering Services (PES) is a professional construction and engineering company based in Nsambya, Makindye, Uganda.

We specialize in delivering high-quality construction, civil engineering, renovation, and project management services while maintaining professional standards, safety, and customer satisfaction.

Led by Engineer Edrine Kalule, our team brings hands-on technical expertise to every project — from residential homes and apartment blocks to road construction and full civil engineering works. We pride ourselves on quality workmanship, transparent communication, and completing projects on time and on budget.
